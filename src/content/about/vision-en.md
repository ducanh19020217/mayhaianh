## Vision

To become a multinational corporation with integrated manufacturing, business and service operations, with garment production as its core activity — establishing the Hai Anh fashion brand in regional and global markets while building a corporate culture that contributes meaningfully to communities.

## Mission

1. Provide high-quality fashion products with a distinctive, refined and modern design style.
2. Establish Hai Anh as a globally recognized brand.
3. Meet the needs and expectations of all staff and shareholders.
4. Spread cultural values to the community.

## Tagline

"Raise the level, share the benefit!"
