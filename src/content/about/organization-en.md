- Planning
- Human resources
- Technical
- QA
- Production

## Production division

- Production director
- Sewing lines
- Machine operators
- Quality inspection
- Cutting
- Finishing

The company currently employs around 6,000 staff, operating factories across Hai Duong, Thai Binh and Thanh Hoa provinces.
