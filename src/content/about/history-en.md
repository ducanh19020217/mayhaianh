## Founding

The company officially began operations on March 17, 2008, under business registration number 0800447164, issued by the Hai Duong Province Department of Planning and Investment.

## Timeline of expansion

- **March 15, 2008**: Factory 1 established in Ba Dong hamlet, Thai Minh commune, Binh Giang district, Hai Duong Province (7,700 m² total area).
- **2009**: Launched production of knitted garments, swimwear, undergarments and fashion items.
- **2010**: Expanded from 15 to 20 sewing lines.
- **2012**: Invested in 2 additional workshop buildings and 25 sewing lines.
- **2016**: Renovated all buildings and expanded support areas.
- **2017**: Scaled from 7,700 m² to 40,000 m² across 5 buildings.
- **2018**: Opened Factory 2 at Hong Phuc Industrial Zone, Hung Long commune, Ninh Giang district, Hai Duong Province — 70,000 m² with 64 sewing lines.
- **2021**: Expanded Factory 2 with a second, two-story workshop block for storage and 75+ sewing lines.
- **2022**: The second workshop block at Hong Phuc became operational, including an accessory warehouse, cutting department, 23 sewing lines and a finishing department.

The company now operates multiple factories across Hai Duong, Thai Binh and Thanh Hoa provinces, producing fashion knitwear, swimwear, undergarments and wool textiles for export orders.
